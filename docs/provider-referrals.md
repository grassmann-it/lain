## Supplying links for Lain

Provide only the shareable referral URL/code and the applicable program terms.
API keys and login credentials are not needed. Public program availability does
not establish approval for app placement. Once approved links are supplied,
label their benefit and referral relationship explicitly; leave ordinary
provider links available. No referral attribution is silently attached to login.

This guide records public research only. No account was opened, application
submitted, invitation sent or purchase made. Current login-only rewards remain
unverified. The dated research reports retain their original verification dates.
