## Reading key and verification limits

- **Evidenced** means a public program, not Tom's acceptance or approval for public placement in lain.
- **Unresolved / unconfirmed** means reliable product-specific evidence is missing; it does not assert that private programs do not exist. General product/partner pages do not evidence API referral compensation.
- **Rechecked 26.10.** marks public sources actually read again. Search-index evidence, login barriers and empty app shells are explicitly identified. **Carried forward from 24.10.** is not a current full verification; these unresolved expansion entries remain visible research gaps.
- Cash commissions, non-withdrawable credit, subscription discounts, chances to win and grant credits are separate benefits. Personal eligibility, region and the specific distribution channel remain unverified for all programs unless explicitly documented publicly.
