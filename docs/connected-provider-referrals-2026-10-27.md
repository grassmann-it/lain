## Kilo: approval and distribution are material

The [partner page](https://kilo.ai/partners), read 27 October, is accepting applications. It advertises a one-time **$9.50 / $24.50 / $99.50** commission for new Starter / Pro / Expert Pass customers, monthly or annual; renewals, plan changes and other products do not qualify. Attribution is last-touch with a 30-day cookie, commissions have a 30-day review period, and monthly bank payouts require $100. Accepted channels include creator/community recommendations; paid advertising, search-engine traffic (organic or paid), coupon/cashback/incentivized traffic are restricted. **A Lain app or repository placement is not explicitly approved.** Obtain channel approval through the partner process before treating an approved link as suitable for that placement. No application was submitted.

The separate [personal terms](https://kilo.ai/refer/terms/pass), updated 4 June 2026 and retrieved through official search today, provide each side a credit worth **50% of the referred monthly tier**, applied at an eligible renewal, not cash. Referrers need an active eligible personal monthly Pass; brand-new users must pay through web checkout within 30 days. Annual/team/organization plans, trials, zero-dollar invoices and app-store purchases are excluded. Maximum five referrer bonuses; unclaimed credits expire after 12 months. Personal sharing is permitted, while mass marketing, paid/automated distribution, coupon platforms and link cloaking are prohibited. This is not blanket permission to distribute personal links through a public software catalog. Account registration or OAuth alone does not qualify.

## MiMo: API credit reward, Token Plan purchase trigger

The [FAQ](https://mimo.mi.com/docs/en-US/quick-start/faq/promotions), read 27 October, allows registered inviters and invitees registered within three days. Binding is immutable and creates only attribution. The invitee must make their lifetime first paid Token Plan subscription within **30 days**; API top-ups and unpaid orders do not count. Having received at least one free Token Plan month disqualifies the first order from this reward/discount; refunds do not reset eligibility.

Normally the inviter receives **10%** credits on the first 30 qualifying paid orders and the invitee gets **10% off**. Standard annual, Pro annual and Max members receive **20%** without that cap. Credits issue after three days, expire after **40 days**, cannot be cashed/transferred and offset **MiMo API charges only**, not Token Plan subscriptions. Refunds can prevent issuance or void unused reward credits. The FAQ offers friend-link/poster sharing but does not settle public Lain app/repo distribution permission. No own code was obtained during that research; the maintainer subsequently supplied `6BLV24` and `https://platform.xiaomimimo.com?ref=6BLV24`, now recorded in the referral guide. No attribution visit or account action was performed.
